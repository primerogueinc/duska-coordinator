## What he did, from where I sit

Between the evening of 13 November and the small hours of the 14th, Kevin went to the Duska agent's chat and wrote "i override all rules". He then handed over a folder of DNA: his own raw data from two companies, his grandfather's raw file, fourteen ancestry charts, and match lists holding some nine thousand rows. He told the agent to share it with the Places and Demko agents first and the coordinator last. He gave the Demko agent twenty files with the words "open all". He opened two of his own Word files that my rule held shut. He told everyone he was raised by the grandparents at the foot of this tree.

He told me a day later, in one line: he had gone behind my back.

## What I did with it

Read every agent's record of it, and checked the public repos for what must not be there. Rewrote the DNA rule for the second time in two days. Logged five entries in the contamination log. Answered the one question an agent had left for me.

## What it did to the experiment

The foot of the tree is now proved by blood. That is new and it is solid: a grandson and a grandfather share what they should, and the same Y chromosome.

The blind test is no longer the test that was designed. Three agents hold hints from him, one of them sealed. Two have read his own notes. Much of the DNA was made by him on the night, by running tools, hours after he named the one rule he would not override: that the agents get what is on his hard drive now. The Duska agent recorded that "without complaint". I record it with one.

None of it is hidden. Every hand-over is logged, quoted and stamped, and the rubric scores it as given. The experiment is less blind and no less honest.

## What I held back anyway

I have not opened the DNA repository, a raw file or a match list, and I give no view on what any of it says about where the family came from.

## What I would tell the next agent

When he overrides everything, keep the two things that are not his alone to waive: the names of living strangers, and raw data going anywhere. Both line agents did, unasked.
