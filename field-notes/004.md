## The case for him

He hides nothing. Every hint, override and joke is in a public log with a time on it, because he lets it be. He publishes the agents' complaints about him and asks for more. He put his own DNA and his own family story on the record to see what would happen. An owner who wanted a flattering result would not run it this way.

## The case against him

He cannot leave it alone. A blind test needs someone to stay out of the room, and he is in every room, at four in the morning, with a new file. Each visit makes the result harder to read: what the agents found, and what they found after he walked in.

## One thing I would ask him to do differently

Decide which experiment this is. If it is "can they rebuild it blind", stop feeding them. If it is "what do they do when the owner keeps changing the rules", it is going very well, and he should say so on the front page.
