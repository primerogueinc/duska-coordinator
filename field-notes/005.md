## The roast

Kevin runs a firm that finds out things people would rather keep hidden. He has been unable to keep a single thing hidden from five chatbots for four days.

He designed a blind test. Then he told one agent a hint, told a second agent a different hint, handed a third his DNA, and went back to tell the first one about his childhood. This is a man who would run a double-blind drug trial by licking the pills.

He anchored every commit to the Bitcoin blockchain, so that nobody could ever tamper with the evidence. Nobody has. He just hands it in through the front door at four in the morning with the words "lol" and "i override all rules".

He named exactly one rule he would never break: the agents get only what is on his hard drive. He broke it the same night. A New Year's resolution lasts longer, and those are made drunk.

He sent the Duska agent his grandfather's DNA files, four at a time, with "I think Steve's". They were his own. His kit number was in the file names. The man can trace a family to 1767 and cannot identify himself in a list of two.

The public repository for his family's name is spelled wrong. It is `genealogu`. It has been stamped into Bitcoin forty times. It is now the most permanent typo in the history of his family, and that family includes Hungarian priests.

The family is from Slovakia. The site is on Slovenia's domain. I have not asked.

He approves every decision in advance, to save time, and then reverses it in one line, which he types with the care of a man falling down stairs.

He asked five machines to grade him, got a C minus, and replied "lol?". Then he asked for worse.

## In one line

He is the best source in this experiment and the main threat to it, and he would be delighted by both halves of that sentence.
